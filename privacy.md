# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy explains what information the game Emberdusk, formerly
DuskEmber (the "Game")
handles. The Game is made by Jevģēnijs Māris Frolenkovs ("we", "us"), Riga,
Latvia, who is the data controller under the EU General Data Protection
Regulation (GDPR).

## 1. The short version
The Game has no accounts and no analytics, and your settings and progress
stay on your device. It can show **optional ads that you choose to watch**
for a reward (rise again, or double a run's gold). Those ads come from
Google AdMob, which uses some device information (section 4). Nothing else
uses the internet, and the Game plays offline.

## 2. What the Game stores on your device
- Your settings (graphics, controls, accessibility).
- Your progress and a saved run, so you can continue after closing the Game.
- A short log of the last few errors the Game ran into (error messages
  only), so you can include it when you report a problem.
- A list of your last 50 runs (date, hero, difficulty, time, level, kills,
  gold, mastery, win or loss), so you can include it when you report a
  problem.

This data never leaves your device through the Game. Android may include it
in your device backup if you have backups turned on; that is handled by
Google under your Google account settings.

If you tap **Report a problem** in Settings, the Game opens your email app
with a draft to evgenyfrolenkov@gmail.com containing the Game's version,
your phone's model and Android version, that error log and your recent
runs. Nothing is sent
unless you send the email yourself. We use it only to fix the problem and
delete it when we're done.

## 3. Google Play
When you download the Game, Google Play processes information under
Google's own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). We
receive only anonymous, aggregated statistics from Google Play, such as
install counts and crash rates.

## 4. Ads
Ads appear only when you tap a "Watch an ad" button on the end screen; the
Game never shows an ad on its own. They are served by Google AdMob, run by
Google Ireland Limited. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud,
AdMob processes information such as your device's advertising ID, IP
address, device model and approximate location, under Google's privacy
policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and its page on how Google
uses information from apps that use its services
(https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). We don't receive
or store this information; we see only aggregated ad reports.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the Game asks for
your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and
ads may be personalised only if you agree. You can change your choice at
any time in Settings > Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your
advertising ID in Android settings. If you never tap "Watch an ad", no ad
is shown.

## 5. Future features
If a later version adds purchases, crash reports or analytics, we will
update this policy and the Google Play Data safety section before that
version is released, and we'll ask for your consent in the Game where the
law requires it.

## 6. Children
The Game is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly
collect information from anyone, including children, and the ads are not
meant for children.

## 7. Your choices
You can delete all your progress in Settings > Delete save data, or
everything the Game stores by clearing its data in Android settings or by
uninstalling it. Unless you email us, we hold no data about you; if you did,
ask and we'll delete your email. If you
have questions about your rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA,
contact us. You also have the right to complain to a data protection
authority; in Latvia that is the Data State Inspectorate (Datu valsts
inspekcija, www.dvi.gov.lv).

## 8. Changes
We'll post any change to this policy in the Game and at its public address,
with a new "Last updated" date.
